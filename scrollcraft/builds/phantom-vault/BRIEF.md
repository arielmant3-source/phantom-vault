# PHANTOM VAULT: brief

Built from the user's written spec, not interviewed. Every topic below quotes or paraphrases that spec;
decisions the spec did not cover are marked **Authored**.

1. **Vibe:** "עיצוב שחור יוקרתי עם אפקטים סגולים", "מראה של מערכת אבטחה מתקדמת". Authored references: a bank vault door at night, a forensic lab report, the film "The Conversation".
2. **Journey (user's sections):** the vault (code, fingerprint, apps, auto-lock, private files), then privacy and hiding options, then the Family Link lab, then install and APK.
3. **Energy:** Authored. Quiet, heavy open; precise middle; the most intense moment is the lab, then a calm, solid close.
4. **Feeling, stage by stage, and the one moment:** see the curve below. Peak: the lab scanner.
5. **What no other site does:** the spec says "אל תציג תוצאה כהצלחה אם היא לא נבדקה בפועל". So the page shows the parent's view honestly and lets the visitor record their own device results; nothing is shown as tested unless the visitor tested it.
6. **Range:** premium-minimal pushed toward forensic/editorial (dense readouts, mono labels).
7. **World:** distinct scenes, not one flight.
8. **Assets:** the existing web vault (`app/`) and the signed APK (`PhantomVault.apk`, SHA-256 in `version.json`). No photography, no generation key: hero planes are drawn in SVG and CSS.

## Ethical frame (fixed)
Family Link is a parent's supervision tool. The page does not offer, describe or link any way to hide apps from a supervising parent. It states what is documented, marks what was not tested, and names the one official path: the parent's choice, or Google's age-of-consent handover.

## Feeling curve
1. Hero: awe, then trust. A vault door turns under the hand, and the second line admits its limits.
2. Contents: calm competence. Four things the vault actually does, plainly.
3. Hide is not isolate: a small jolt. Three lines assemble the distinction.
4. Options compared: clarity. Rows wipe in, each with a verdict.
5. **Peak, Family Link lab:** exposure, then relief at the honesty. The scan beam still finds the hidden app.
6. Test protocol: agency. The visitor records results; defaults are "not tested".
7. Close: resolve. Open the vault.

**Peak:** "There's a scanner that sweeps the phone and shows you exactly what your parent's app still sees, even after you hide the icon." Act 5.

**Tell-someone sentence:** It's the site where the vault tells you the truth about what it cannot hide.
